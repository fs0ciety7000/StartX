A 19th-century steel engraving / etching, pure white ink lines on a deep black background, night scene, very fine cross-hatching, high contrast, monochrome, no text, no lettering, no signature, no border.
Wide landscape. A crossroads on an open plain at night: on the right third stands a tall old weathered wooden signpost with many arrow-shaped boards pointing in different directions (the boards are blank, no letters), an oil lantern hanging from it glowing brightly and lighting the ground around. Several dirt roads radiate from the crossroads toward distant destinations on the horizon: a far mountain range on the left, a small city skyline with towers and a domed observatory in the centre distance, a lighthouse on a coastal cliff on the far right with a faint beam. A traveller with a backpack and a walking staff stands at the foot of the signpost looking up at it. Dry grass, stones and wild shrubs in the foreground, a winding path coming from the bottom left. Starry sky with a few constellations, large empty black sky in the upper half.
Style matching Gustave Doré engravings, detailed, atmospheric, cinematic composition.
